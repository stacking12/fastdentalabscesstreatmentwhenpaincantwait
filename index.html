Dental Abscess Treatment

Dental Abscess Treatment in Marietta, GA

A dental abscess is a pocket of infection that forms around a tooth or in the gums. It won't go away on its own, and waiting can allow the infection to spread. Our dental abscess treatment focuses on relieving your pain fast and clearing the infection at its source.

Common signs of a dental abscess

  • Severe, throbbing or constant toothache
  • Swollen gums, cheek or jaw
  • A pimple-like bump on the gum
  • Bad taste or odor in the mouth
  • Pain when chewing or with hot and cold
  • Fever or tender glands
📞 Call for Abscess Treatment
Go to the ER immediately if you have swelling that is spreading, trouble breathing or swallowing, or a high fever.

Dental Abscess & Emergency Dental Questions

What is dental abscess treatment?

Dental abscess treatment removes the source of a tooth or gum infection. Depending on your case, this can include draining the abscess, a root canal to clean the infected tooth, or an extraction, often alongside medication as directed by your dentist.

How do I know if I have a dental abscess?

Common signs include severe or throbbing tooth pain, swelling in the gum or face, a pimple-like bump on the gum, a bad taste in the mouth, sensitivity to hot or cold, and sometimes fever. A dentist needs to examine you to confirm the cause.

Is a dental abscess a dental emergency?

Yes. An abscess will not heal on its own and the infection can spread. Call us for an urgent appointment. If you have facial swelling that is spreading, trouble breathing or swallowing, or a high fever, go to the nearest emergency room immediately.

Can I get a same day dentist appointment for a toothache?

We aim to see urgent patients as quickly as possible. Call 678-370-7746 and describe your symptoms so we can arrange the soonest available emergency visit.

How much does emergency dental care cost?
